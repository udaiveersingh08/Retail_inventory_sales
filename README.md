# 🛒 Retail Inventory & Sales Analysis

## 📌 1. Project Overview

The **Retail Inventory & Sales Analysis** project focuses on analyzing retail sales and inventory data to evaluate overall business performance, product profitability, store-level sales, supplier contribution, and inventory availability.

This project analyzes 2,800 transaction records across five retail stores, ten products, and four product categories during the year 2025.

The main objective is to transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights, identify sales and profitability trends, evaluate inventory performance, and support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 3. Project Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales and revenue performance.

- Analyze supplier-wise sales and profitability.
- Calculate gross profit and gross profit margins.
- Identify low-stock products and inventory risks.
- Analyze monthly and seasonal sales trends.

- Generate actionable business insights for better decision-making.
